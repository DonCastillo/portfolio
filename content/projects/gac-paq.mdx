summary: A multilingual research app that lets participants around the world complete a physical activity questionnaire on their phone, with an admin portal for the research team.
problem: >-
  About 80% of young people worldwide aren't active enough, yet there's no
  reliable way to compare their activity across countries. Existing
  questionnaires were built for high-income countries, miss activities like
  active play, and say little about young children, so researchers in low- and
  middle-income countries had no tool that fit their participants.
solution: >-
  The study is led by the University of Lethbridge with more than 25
  universities and public health organizations worldwide. For them, I built a
  cross-platform app that delivers the new GAC-PAQ questionnaire to children,
  teens and parents in each country's own languages, works offline in rural and
  low-connectivity areas, and sends every response to one admin portal, so the
  team can validate the questionnaire against accelerometer data across 17
  countries.
results:
  - Carried both the pilot and the main study in every country, with 24k+ responses from 5,400+ participants so far and 99% of submissions received, online and offline.
  - Localized wording, images and UI in each version make the questionnaire culturally familiar, so participants, especially non-English speakers, can understand and answer it.
  - Audio narration lets young children who can't read yet and participants with low vision complete the questionnaire on their own.
  - Research teams in all 17 countries monitor progress and export their own data from the admin portal, without developer help.
  - Shipped 5 major versions through rounds of closed testing with 30–40 remote testers, fixing issues before the main study launched.
