summary: A one-minute, multi-scene animation in Processing that follows a UFO from deep space to a farm on Earth, timed scene by scene with my own vector art and sound.
problem: >-
  The brief started from Paul Klee's idea of "a dot that went for a walk": an
  animation of at least four timed scenes, under a minute long, that uses
  movement, pacing and composition to change how we see its objects.
solution: >-
  An animation that zooms from the scale of space down to a single farmhouse. A
  UFO dodges asteroids, crosses in front of the Earth, beams over a city
  skyline, and lands in a field, where its aliens walk off toward a barn. Each
  scene is timed to the second, with sound effects to match.

highlights:
  - Storyboarded all seven scenes by hand first, noting when each element enters and leaves, then built them to that timing with Processing's millis().
  - Drew the vector art myself, including the alien with its walk-cycle frames and the spaceship, and layered it over photo backgrounds.
  - Modelled the moving objects with a base class and subclasses for the alien, spaceship and asteroids, so each shares position and movement but animates its own way.
  - Synced sound effects to the action, from the spaceship's hum to the landing, the door and the footsteps.
