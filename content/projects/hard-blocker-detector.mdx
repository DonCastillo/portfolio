title: Hard Blocker Detector

category: hackathon
summary: An AI tool that reads a job posting against your profile and flags any hard rule that disqualifies you, quoting the sentence that proves it, before you spend an hour applying.
problem: >-
  A tailored job application takes 30–40 minutes, and much of that goes to
  postings the applicant was never eligible for. The disqualifying rule is
  stated once, often indirectly: "no visa sponsorship" in the benefits footer,
  or "must live near our Austin office" under a header that says Remote. Triaging
  ten postings by hand took 16 minutes, and a third of them had a hard blocker.
solution: >-
  A command-line tool that makes one Claude call per posting. It checks 14 kinds
  of hard blocker, quotes the exact sentence for each one it finds, and drops
  any quote that isn't actually in the posting. The verdict, SKIP or APPLY,
  sits beside the line of your profile it conflicts with.
results:
  - Showed that a one-sentence definition for each of the 14 blocker types was worth 0.130 of the 0.155 F1 gain; every more elaborate agent technique tried after it was measured and removed.
  - Requiring a quoted sentence added nothing to F1 but cut human review from 97 to 14 seconds a posting, because each verdict can be checked without reopening the posting.
  - Kept the result honest by freezing the evaluation protocol before writing the system; all four later amendments made the result less impressive, not more.

highlights:
  - Froze the evaluation protocol before writing the system, with 24 seeded synthetic postings whose blockers are planted at exact character spans, plus 8 clean postings with near-misses so that flagging everything can't score well.
  - Built a one-call Claude pipeline that returns each blocker with its verbatim evidence, then a free, mechanical grounding check that drops any quote not found in the posting, taking hallucinated evidence to zero.
  - Ran the baseline and final system three times each to measure the noise floor (0.061 F1), so no gain smaller than run-to-run variation is claimed.
  - Built, measured and removed four more elaborate designs (per-group decomposition and two verification passes), each documented in the changelog with what it cost.
  - Directed Claude Code (Claude Opus 5) through 115 turns to write all of the code, tests and documentation, and submitted the full development transcript.
