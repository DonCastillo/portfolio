title: Visualizing 8 Billion

category: academic
summary: A one-page scrolling infographic on the world reaching 8 billion people, told through expressive typography, variable fonts and scroll-triggered animation.
problem: >-
  When the world population reached 8 billion in November 2022, the trends
  behind it (slowing growth, falling birth rates, longer lives, an ageing
  population) were mostly reported as long text and tables, which are easy to
  skim past and hard to remember.
solution: >-
  A single scrolling page that turns each trend into a typographic composition.
  Font weight, width and size carry the meaning of each fact, icons count out
  the numbers, and animations reveal each section as the reader scrolls.

highlights:
  - Designed a one-page infographic on six population trends, from how long each billion took to how fast the world is ageing, using figures from the UN Population Fund, the World Economic Forum and Visual Capitalist.
  - Made typography the main visual, setting variable fonts (Roboto Flex, and Lab DJR used with its designer's permission) at different widths, weights and grades so each phrase looks like what it says.
  - Built the billion-by-billion timeline from JSON with jQuery, drawing a bar and a row of people icons for each billion.
  - Triggered the animations on scroll with AOS and IntersectionObserver, so bars grow, statistics slide in and the population pyramid builds one step at a time.
  - Laid out the continents as a photo mosaic, from Asia at 4.7 billion to Oceania at 44 million.
