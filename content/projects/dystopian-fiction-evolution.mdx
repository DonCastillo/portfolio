summary: An interactive timeline of dystopian fiction from the 1900s to the 2010s, showing how the real events of each era shaped the stories people told.
problem: >-
  Dystopian fiction tracks the fears of its time, from fascism in 1984 to
  surveillance and conformity in The Hunger Games, but that link between real
  history and fiction is hard to see when the books are read one at a time.
solution: >-
  A single scrolling page that defines six common dystopian themes, then walks
  through five eras. Each era pairs the real events of the period with the books
  it produced, and every book is tagged with the themes it explores.

highlights:
  - Researched dystopian fiction across five eras, from the genre's start in the 1900s to the young-adult wave of the 2010s, and linked each era's themes to its real events, such as World War II, the Moon landing and HIV/AIDS.
  - Designed an icon for each of six themes, from oppressive government to cyberpunk, and tagged every book with the themes it explores, so the shift between eras shows up as a change in icons.
  - Built the timeline from JSON with jQuery, rendering each era's title screen, story, notable events and book cards from the data.
  - Added scroll-triggered animation with the AOS library, and credited every image and source on a separate page.
