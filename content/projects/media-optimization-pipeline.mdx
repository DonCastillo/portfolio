summary: A media setup for client websites that routes CMS uploads to the right storage, optimizes assets on the frontend, and keeps files out of the site repos.
problem: >-
  Images and files are usually the heaviest part of a content-driven website.
  The goal was to make client sites load faster, keep media available
  independently of the CMS, and keep site repos lean as content grew.
solution: >-
  A CMS extension that decides where each upload is stored, frontend modules
  that optimize assets, and a build step that keeps media out of the site repos.
results:
  - Media is served separately from the CMS, so sites keep showing images if the CMS is unavailable.
  - Pages load faster, including on slow connections, now that assets are a smaller share of page weight.
  - Media delivery became more efficient, with smaller files served from a CDN.
  - Site repos stay small and build faster without media files committed to them.
