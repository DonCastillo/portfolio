summary: A Who Wants to Be a Millionaire–style trivia game built on the Open Trivia Database, with a Pig Latin mode that doubles the prize money and adds lifelines of its own.
problem: >-
  The assignment was to take data from a public REST API and show it "in an
  unexpected way", giving it new meaning in a single-page site whose design
  supports the concept. A plain list of trivia questions would meet the
  technical brief but not the creative one.
solution: >-
  A game-show quiz that turns Open Trivia Database questions into a 15-step
  climb to a million dollars, with lifelines and a public scoreboard. In Pig
  Latin mode every question and answer is translated, the prizes double, and two
  extra lifelines help you decode them.

highlights:
  - Built the game in SvelteKit and TypeScript, pulling multiple-choice questions from the Open Trivia Database with a session token so a game never repeats a question.
  - Added a Pig Latin mode that translates every question and answer, doubles the money tree, and adds two lifelines of its own, Unpiglatinized (translate back to English) and Ask the Pig (the right answer, in Pig Latin).
  - Implemented the classic lifelines too, 50/50, Ask the Audience with simulated votes that aren't always right, and Switch Question.
  - Saved finished games to a MongoDB scoreboard through Realm Web, with a page for each score.
  - Recreated the show's look with Tailwind CSS, from the angled answer panels to the 15-step money tree and its milestone rounds.
